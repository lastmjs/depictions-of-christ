About this collection

This archive is generated from Wikidata — every item that depicts Jesus Christ — or a scene in which he appears, such as the Nativity, the Crucifixion or the Madonna and Child — and that has an image on Wikimedia Commons. It is joined by public-domain records from the open-access programs of the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the National Gallery of Art in Washington, the Art Institute of Chicago, the Cleveland Museum of Art and the Rijksmuseum, and by the Web Gallery of Art, with duplicates removed where they can be detected. Commons hosts only public-domain or freely licensed media, and the museum images are released as public domain (CC0). Dates marked “c.” without a precise record are estimated from the artist's lifetime. Information is only as complete as the catalogue; if you spot an error, you can correct it on Wikidata for everyone.
