About this collection

This archive is generated from Wikidata — every item that depicts Jesus Christ — or a scene in which he appears, such as the Nativity, the Crucifixion or the Madonna and Child — and that has an image on Wikimedia Commons. It is joined by records from the open-access programs of the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the National Gallery of Art in Washington, the Art Institute of Chicago, the Cleveland Museum of Art, the J. Paul Getty Museum, the Walters Art Museum, the Yale University Art Gallery and Yale Center for British Art, the Princeton University Art Museum, the Victoria and Albert Museum, the Rijksmuseum, SMK – the National Gallery of Denmark, the Nationalmuseum in Stockholm and the Finnish National Gallery; by nearly three hundred European museums and libraries through Europeana; by the Web Gallery of Art; and by works found through dedicated research, with duplicates removed where they can be detected. Works are being hand-checked century by century — those marked with a check have been individually reviewed. Commons hosts only public-domain or freely licensed media, the museum images are public domain or openly licensed (each image shows its credit and licence; Victoria and Albert Museum images are free for non-commercial use). Dates marked “c.” without a precise record are estimated from the artist's lifetime. Information is only as complete as the catalogue; if you spot an error, you can correct it on Wikidata for everyone.
