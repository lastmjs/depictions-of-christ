The Face of Christ

“For God, who commanded the light to shine out of darkness, hath shined in our hearts, to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.”2 Corinthians 4:6

For nearly two thousand years, believers have painted, carved, stitched and built the image of their Lord. Walk with them, century by century — from the hidden walls of the catacombs to the mountaintops of the modern world.

The Image Continues

“Wherefore God also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above every name: that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow.”Philippians 2:9–10

No single image could ever contain him, and the Church has never claimed one does. Yet each of these works is an act of love — an attempt by a particular person, in a particular place and time, to say who Jesus is. The story they tell together is still being written.
