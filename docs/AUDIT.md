# Robustness audit — status by century

The audit proceeds one century at a time. A century is **complete** only when all three passes are done:

1. **Completeness** — specialist research has searched for every known surviving depiction of Christ from that
   century (by medium and region) that has a freely licensed image online, and added any that were missing.
2. **Hand-check** — every work has been individually reviewed (image viewed, Christ confirmed, catalogue facts
   verified, duplicates removed) and given a summary, scripture and facts. See `docs/ENRICH.md`.
3. **Categorisation** — subject, form, date, place of creation and map placement confirmed.

## 1st–10th centuries — ✅ complete (as far as freely licensed images exist)

- **982 works** in the collection for this period, every one individually hand-checked (up from 145 unverified works
  when the audit began).
- **Completeness research** added 853 works the harvests had missed: 126 catacomb paintings, sarcophagi and gold glass;
  120 mosaics and wall paintings; 366 manuscript miniatures (incl. the complete Stuttgart Psalter and every Christ
  drawing of the Utrecht Psalter); 153 ivories, metalwork, coins, seals, icons, crosses and textiles.
- **The undated section was triaged** for early works: 14 pre-1000 works found there and moved to their centuries;
  281 later works dated.
- **Removed** across the audit: duplicates, works that do not depict Christ, later reproductions (casts,
  electrotypes) and one counterfeit icon.
- **Systematic data fixes** found by the audit: catalogue dates like "4th quarter 14th century", "184-", "German 19th
  Century" were being misread; all such dates are now parsed correctly across the whole collection.
